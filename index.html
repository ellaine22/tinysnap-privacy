Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 7, 2026

PuzzlePop is a casual puzzle game designed for users who enjoy simple, relaxing puzzle experiences.

Information We Collect

PuzzlePop does not collect, store, or share personal information.

The app does not require an account, login, or user registration.

Advertising

PuzzlePop does not display advertisements.

No third-party advertising SDKs are included in the app.

In-App Purchases

PuzzlePop includes an optional one-time in-app purchase that unlocks the full collection of puzzles.

Purchases are processed through Apple's App Store. PuzzlePop does not collect or store your payment or billing information.

Once the full version is unlocked, additional puzzle packs and categories added to the full collection may be made available without requiring a subscription.

Third-Party Services

PuzzlePop may use third-party services solely to support app functionality, such as managing in-app purchase entitlements.

These services may process limited technical information necessary to provide their functionality. PuzzlePop does not use this information for advertising or to create advertising profiles.

Data Tracking

PuzzlePop does not track users across apps or websites for advertising purposes.

The app does not use third-party advertising or behavioral tracking technologies.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to PuzzlePop or its functionality.

Any updates will be published on this page along with a revised "Last updated" date.
